MY MUSIC.

Music is the center of my creative world. I combine electronic production, experimental sound design, melodic songwriting, and energetic rhythms to create my own sound.

My production process often starts with a melody, a drum pattern, or an interesting synthesizer sound. From there, I build arrangements using bass, electronic drums, vocal layers, and different sound textures.

FEATURED PROJECT

HEARTBROKEN ELECTRONIC

Heartbroken Electronic is a concept project about expressing heartbreak through electronic music. It combines emotional songwriting with distorted synthesizers, heavy bass, electronic drums, and processed vocals.

The idea is to express complicated emotions through sounds that can be both beautiful and chaotic.
